{{NAME}} {{VERSION}} for macOS
=======================================

This is a beta: thank you for trying it. Please tell us what goes wrong (see "Reporting a problem" below).
It runs on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 11 or later.


Starting it
-----------

1. Double-click the zip to unpack it (Safari may already have done this for you).

2. Drag "{{NAME}}" to your Applications folder (or anywhere you like).

3. Double-click it. The first time, macOS will stop it, because the beta isn't notarized by Apple yet
   (that needs a paid developer account). Nothing is wrong with it. To let it run:

   macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later:
     a. Double-click the app. A message says it can't be opened, or was blocked. Click "Done" (not
        "Move to Trash").
     b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
     c. Next to '"{{NAME}}" was blocked...', click "Open Anyway", then confirm with your password.

   macOS 14 (Sonoma) or earlier:
     Right-click (or Control-click) the app, choose "Open", then click "Open" in the box that appears.

   macOS remembers your choice, so this happens only once.

   If you're comfortable with Terminal, this does the same in one step (adjust the path if you put the
   app somewhere else):
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/{{NAME}}.app"


Where your settings are kept
----------------------------

Settings, workspaces and macros are saved in:
    ~/Library/Application Support/Pangolin/{{APP}}
(In Finder: Go > Go to Folder..., then paste that path.)

Your pictures are saved wherever you choose when you save them.


Drawing tablets
---------------

For pen pressure and the eraser end with a Wacom tablet, install Wacom's driver for macOS (from wacom.com)
and restart the app. Other tablets need their maker's macOS driver too.


Updating or removing it
-----------------------

To update, replace the app with the new version (your settings stay where they are). To remove it, move
the app to the Trash; delete the folder above too if you want your settings gone.


Reporting a problem
-------------------

Open an issue at https://github.com/BrandonHilde/{{APP}}/issues and include:

  - your macOS version and Mac model (Apple menu > About This Mac),
  - the version from Help > About in the app (it looks like "{{VERSION}} (1a2b3c4)"),
  - what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead (a screenshot helps),
  - if it's about pictures not looking right or crashes, the output of the self test, from Terminal:
        "/Applications/{{NAME}}.app/Contents/MacOS/{{APP}}" --selftest
